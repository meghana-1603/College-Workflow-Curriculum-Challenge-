## 2. Requirement-to-evidence method

For each requirement, compare the official course description with at least one actual assessment or rubric.

- **Present and assessed:** Cite the course and assessment; recommend KEEP if the depth is adequate.
- **Present but mostly theoretical or lightly practised:** Identify the missing assessed task and recommend UPDATE.
- **No evidence of coverage:** Consider ADD or an approved elective/embedded assessment after checking the complete syllabus.
- **Not checked:** Mark “verification pending”; do not classify the skill as absent.
