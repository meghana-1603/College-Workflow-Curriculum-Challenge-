# Individual Contribution and Limitations

## 1. Individual Contribution

- **Curriculum mapping:** Prepared a semester-wise map of course codes, titles and credits using my academic record.
- **Curriculum review:** Consulted official IFHE curriculum and syllabus references to inform the proposed changes.
- **Job-description analysis:** Compared AI engineering and data engineering job requirements with relevant existing courses.
- **AI-assisted exercises:** Prepared exercises covering Java programming, SQL, machine learning evaluation, data wrangling and grounded question answering.
- **Interactive comparison:** Developed an HTML-based course comparison tool with search, filters and a credit-impact calculator.
- **Workflow investigation:** Documented the Game Development Club event-approval workflow and proposed a spreadsheet-based tracker and related test cases.
- **Documentation:** Prepared the curriculum recommendations, credit-impact plan, supporting evidence and limitations.

## 2. Limitations

1. The course map requires confirmation against the official curriculum and regulations applicable to the 2023–2027 batch.
2. Semester VII course codes and placement, and the Semester VIII internship code and title, require confirmation.
3. The three job postings are illustrative examples, not a representative labour-market survey. The two Optum postings have overlapping requirements.
4. AI-assisted exercises do not establish proficiency in every relevant skill or replace formal course assessments.
5. Faculty and recent-graduate feedback was not obtained or documented.
6. No measured time savings or real operational impact is claimed for the proposed workflow tracker.
7. All proposed curriculum changes are student recommendations, not official ICFAI decisions.
8. The affected-credit calculation represents credits associated with courses proposed for assessment updates, not newly added or removed credits.
9. Repository, commit and release links will be included in the final submission documentation.

## 3. Conclusion

This proposal identifies potential opportunities to strengthen practical assessment in the B.Tech Data Science and Artificial Intelligence curriculum. The recommendations draw on the available academic record, public job descriptions, proposed practical exercises and the workflow-improvement case study. Any implementation would require review against the applicable curriculum and institutional regulations.
